### Selected work

**[Kanoun](https://github.com/YahiaKerroum/Kanoun)**: a restaurant operating system with QR table ordering, live kitchen tickets, payments and a back office. It ships as a Windows desktop app on PostgreSQL. *I built it alone.*

**[Qima](https://qima-kappa.vercel.app) (قيمة)**: fair asking prices for used laptops in Algeria, learned from Ouedkniss listings. Every estimate comes with an explanation, and a deals finder flags underpriced listings. *I led the team, worked on the models, and designed and built the platform.* [Code](https://github.com/khalilgh1/laptop-price-prediction)

**[FPL Assistant](https://fpl-assistant-ten.vercel.app)**: a Fantasy Premier League desk with points forecasts, a squad optimiser and transfer and captaincy advice. *I was the main contributor: models, platform design and part of the build.*

**[ClinicPulse](https://github.com/YahiaKerroum/Dentist-Management-System)**: an operating system for dental practices, with a live daily dashboard, per-tooth records, appointments, finances and role permissions. *I co-led the original system, then rebuilt the whole platform.*

**[DzairAI](https://dzair.ai)**: a national platform for Algeria's AI ecosystem, covering experts, companies, datasets, events and news, with CV parsing and job matching. *I was one of the leads.*

**[Adaptive Quiz Platform](https://github.com/YahiaKerroum/QuizPlatform)**: quizzes that pick the next question from how the learner is doing, and estimate their level from as few questions as possible. *I was one of the leads.*

**[FinDar](https://fin-dar-website.vercel.app)**: a Flutter real-estate app for Algeria with listings, search, favourites and boosts. *I was a main contributor and did all of the localization.*
